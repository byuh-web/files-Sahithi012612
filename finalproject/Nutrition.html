Fruit Nutrition

Fruits are naturally rich in vitamins, minerals, fiber, and antioxidants that help keep our bodies healthy. Different fruits contain different nutrients, so eating a variety of fruits every day can provide many health benefits. They help improve digestion, strengthen the immune system, support heart health, and provide natural energy for everyday activities.
