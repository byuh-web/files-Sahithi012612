Easy Fruit Recipes

Fruits are not only healthy to eat on their own, but they can also be used to make delicious meals and snacks. Below are a few simple recipes that anyone can prepare at home using fresh fruits.

Fruit Salad

Mix together chopped strawberries, blueberries, mangoes, bananas, pineapples, and grapes. Add a little honey and fresh lemon juice for extra flavor. Chill for 30 minutes before serving.

Berry Smoothie

Blend one cup of blueberries, strawberries, one banana, one cup of milk, and a few ice cubes until smooth. This smoothie is refreshing and full of vitamins.

Fruit Yogurt Parfait

Layer Greek yogurt with strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, and granola. Repeat the layers and serve immediately for a healthy breakfast or dessert.
