About Badminton

Badminton is a fast-paced sport played by millions of people around the world. The game is played using rackets and a shuttlecock on a rectangular court. Players must stay focused and active during matches because the shuttlecock moves very quickly. Badminton can be played as singles or doubles, making it both a competitive and team-based sport. Many people enjoy badminton because it is fun and exciting for players of all ages.
