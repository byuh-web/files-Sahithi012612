About Chiang Mai

Chiang Mai is one of the most beautiful cities in Northern Thailand. It is famous for temples, mountains, night markets, elephant sanctuaries, and peaceful culture.

Chiang Mai is also known for its relaxing atmosphere, traditional Thai culture, and beautiful nature.
